Options

Fine-tune diffuser and nightlight behavior.

Pura syncs the nightlight with the diffuser. Turn this on to keep the nightlight off when the diffuser is running.
Replaces the on/off switch with a fan accessory to control intensity (Subtle, Medium, Strong). For multi-bay diffusers, HomeKit intensity changes are applied across available bays to keep auto-alternate behavior consistent. Changing this may require recreating HomeKit scenes or automations for all Pura diffusers in this plugin.
Enables nightlight controls (On, Brightness, and Color) for compatible diffusers.
Separate Tile creates a dedicated Nightlight accessory, while Single Tile combines it with the diffuser; changing this may require updating scenes or automations.
